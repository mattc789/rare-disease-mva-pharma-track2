## What the pitch presents

The video proposes **bortezomib** and **trametinib** as distinct, literature-supported *research hypotheses* for evaluation in the context of Mosaic Variegated Aneuploidy (MVA). The presentation outlines:

1. The patient-first interpretation of the authorized biological and genomic evidence;
2. A reproducible software workflow, mechanism-first drug screening, and source-aware evidence checks;
3. Experimental observations supporting the exploratory LZTR1 abundance hypothesis for bortezomib and downstream signaling hypothesis for trametinib;
4. The disease-specific causal and functional questions requiring independent preclinical testing.

These are research hypotheses for further investigation, **not** evidence that either medicine treats MVA and **not** a recommendation to administer any drug to the participant. The full scientific report discusses supporting experiments, contradictory evidence, uncertainties, and proposed test and rejection criteria.
