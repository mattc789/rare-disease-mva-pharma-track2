# Before Installation — Important Notes for the Judging Panel

Please read these notes before installing or running **Rare Disease MVA Pharma — V3.1 Challenge Edition**.

1. **Allow time for installation and research.** Installing the application, downloading and verifying up-to-date public scientific databases, and completing the full genomic and pharmacological analysis may take **up to approximately seven hours**. Actual duration depends on the speed of the Internet connection, the availability and responsiveness of external data providers, and the hardware performance of the computer. Seven hours is an indicative planning estimate, not a guaranteed maximum.

2. **OpenAI API costs observed in our runs.** In our internal development and validation runs to date, the OpenAI API credit consumed was **less than USD 1 per run**. This is the project team's observed expenditure, **not a guaranteed price or billing cap**. Actual costs may differ with model pricing, configuration, and the amount of analysis performed. A reviewer must provide their own valid OpenAI API key.

3. **Scientific results may evolve over time.** The results and proposed drug-repurposing hypotheses may change between executions as new scientific publications become available and as official reference databases of approved medicinal products are updated across the geographical/regulatory jurisdictions considered by the software. The software records source provenance to help interpret differences between runs. All proposed medicines remain **research hypotheses requiring independent experimental validation**, not treatment recommendations.

For system requirements, installation instructions, data-source recovery, and result verification, see the [README](README.md).
